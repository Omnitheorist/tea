§2Chemistry of rejection

Why most tea gets pushed away

Bitterness comes from caffeine and gallated catechins firing the hTAS2R bitter receptors (EGCG and ECG hit hTAS2R39 and hTAS2R14). Astringency is a touch sensation, not a taste. Polyphenols cross-link with proline-rich proteins in saliva, strip the lubricating mucin film off the mouth, and raise friction across the tongue.

The chart shows dose over threshold (DOT) in a standard infusion. Anything right of the dashed line is a compound you can actually taste.

The counter-moveL-theanine

Umami, brothy and mellowing. It dampens the harshness of EGCG and caffeine and amplifies glutamate on the T1R1/T1R3 umami receptor.

The ruleP/A < 3.0

Keep polyphenols under three times the amino acids and panels move from “harsh, medicinal” to “round, luxurious”.

The precedent25–35 mg/g

Theanine in shaded Gyokuro and Tencha, against under 10 mg/g in ordinary sun-grown leaf.

§5From bush to pearl

Half oolong, half breakfast black, frozen at 32%

The leaf has to behave like an aromatic mountain oolong and a comforting black tea at once. The trick is to oxidise just far enough to build bright, sweet theaflavins, then kill the enzyme before the dark, astringent thearubigins form.

  1. 1

    Plucking

    bud + 2 tender leaves

    Only the youngest growth, where theanine and aroma precursors concentrate.

  2. 2

    Withering

    solar, then indoor · 60% moisture loss

    Wakes up the glycosidase enzymes that will free aroma.

  3. 3

    Wave bruising 摇青

    4 cycles · 15–45 min

    Tossing cracks the leaf edges and releases linalool and geraniol from their sugar-bound precursors.

  4. 4

    Controlled oxidation

    25 °C · 88% RH · 110 min · 32%

    Polyphenol oxidase turns catechins into theaflavin-3-gallate: orange-red, bright and sweet.

  5. 5

    Fixation 杀青

    240 °C drum · 120 s

    Heat destroys the enzyme and locks oxidation at the golden-amber point.

  6. 6

    Ball rolling 包揉

    cloth-wrapped, repeated

    Presses the leaf into tight pearls and coats the surface in its own dried sap.

  7. 7

    Two-stage bake

    100 °C dry · 82 °C over fruitwood charcoal

    The charcoal stage drives Strecker reactions that make pyrazines: warm toasted-biscuit notes.

Vanillin and γ-decalactone reach the brain through the nose, not the tongue. Learned association makes the drink taste sweeter with no sugar added.

§9What it does to you

Relaxed alertness, by ratio

L-theanine · 89 mg
One dot = 1 mg, per 250 mL cup.

Theanine crosses the blood-brain barrier, tempers glutamate signalling and raises GABA and dopamine in the prefrontal cortex. At this ratio the target is sustained alpha-band activity (8–12 Hz): awake and calm, without jitters or an afternoon crash.

Meanwhile vanillin and β-ionone travel the olfactory route straight into the orbitofrontal cortex and limbic system, where smell and memory live. Warmth, safety, a kitchen from childhood.
