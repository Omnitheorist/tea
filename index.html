01 · The science of yum

Liking is balance, not intensity.

Consumer + descriptive studies converge: sweet / mild / fruity / floral / harmonious wins. Bitter, astringent, powdery, pungent-fermented loses.

+ like driver

Sweet, mild, fruit-jelly, flower

Highest-liked in n=93 blended-tea test: chamomile, rooibos-strawberry, peach-oolong. PLSR shows floral-fruity volatiles — linalool, geraniol, nerolidol, phenylethanol, jasmine lactone, β-ionone — drive liking.

− dislike driver

Bitter + astringent + powdery

From caffeine + EGCG/ECG + theasinensins. Thresholds: caffeine 500, EGCG 190, ECG 180 μmol/L. Only 30.8% find astringency Just-Right. Too much = −1.39 liking points.

+ umami antidote

Theanine rescues the cup

Theanine + glutamate inhibit bitter presentation. Good greens: caffeine/theanine ~2.8, low P/A ratio. Gyokuro 2500mg/100g theanine vs hojicha 22mg. Shade 20–30 days to boost it.

+ colour, body, huigan

Bright, thick, sweet-return

Liked: transparent yellow-gold, medium body from pectins/theaflavins, clean sweet aftertaste. Disliked: dark over-extracted, drying pucker. Brew 70–80°C 2–3 min; cold-brew = sweetest.

05 · Brew lab

One leaf, three rituals.

80°C · 2.5 min

3g / 250mL. Amino acids out, tannins stay in.

  1. Warm vessel, add leaf + osmanthus.
  2. Pour 80°C, steep 2–3 min, no squeeze.
  3. Sip at 60–65°C for max retronasal honey-rose.
96°C · milky

4g / 200mL + 80mL milk + 7g sugar + cardamom/ginger optional.

  1. Boil leaf hard 3 min to extract theaflavins.
  2. Add milk + sugar + spice, simmer 1 min.
  3. Strain. Malt + cream, no bitterness.
6h · cold

5g / 1L cold-brew 4–8°C, lemon + peach to taste, boba-ready.

  1. Cold-brew overnight — sweetest, smoothest.
  2. Serve over ice + lemon wedge.
  3. Boba variant: + milk + 50% sugar syrup.
